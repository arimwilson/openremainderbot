# Newsletter outlines

Voice: short sentences, one idea per paragraph, no exclamation marks. Sign off "Sam".
About 600 words. Subscribers are salon owners, mostly one to three groomers.

## #14: The no-show nobody meant (due Oct 20)

- Most no-shows aren't rude. They're forgetful.
- What I see across Fernhill salons: reminders the evening before cut no-shows a lot.
  Use real numbers if we have them; don't invent any.
- Three things to try this month: a reminder at 5pm the day before, a 15-minute buffer
  after first-time clients, a deposit only for repeat no-shows.
- Close: ask readers to reply with their no-show policy. I'll share the best ones.
