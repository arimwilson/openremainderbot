# GOALS

Fernhill is my booking app for dog groomers. I build and sell it alone. Sign drafts "Sam".

## Priorities

What these deliverables are for, in tiers, then in order within a tier. A modest
artifact in a high tier beats an impressive one in a low tier.

**(a) Get Fernhill to 50 paying salons by June.** 19 paying and 6 in trial today.

1. Find out why salons that finish the trial don't pay. The latest trial export is
   `notes/trials.csv`.
2. Ship the two features customers ask for most (see `issues.md`, label `customer`).

**(b) Grow the audience that brings in trials.**

3. A newsletter post every two weeks on running a grooming business. Outlines are in
   `notes/newsletter.md`.
4. Talks and guest posts where salon owners gather.

**(c) Everything else.**

5. Internal tools that save me an hour a week.
6. Learning: Postgres performance, pricing.

  every change there myself.
